# Ojai Valley Zen Center

*A quiet place for meditation and practice.*

## Our Practice

Ojai Valley Zen Center was established following the instruction of our teacher, Nyogen Roshi. We practiced together under his guidance for more than twenty years.

We continue to meet for zazen and Zen practice in the Ojai Valley. We welcome anyone interested in Zen meditation and practice, whether experienced or completely new to Zen.

## Zazen

Zazen is the heart of Zen practice.

Beginners are always welcome. Instruction is available, and cushions and benches are provided. Please wear comfortable clothing and arrive about 10-15 minutes before the sitting begins.

After the morning sittings, we usually share coffee and conversation. It is an opportunity to get to know one another and to talk informally about practice and the Dharma.
