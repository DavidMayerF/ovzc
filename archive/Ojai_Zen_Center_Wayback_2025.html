Get started now

There is nothing more important you can do for yourself than to turn the light inward, wake up to your true nature. Here you can sit in the silent stillness of the Ojai mountains, and go deep into the essence of your being. Beginners are welcome. We will introduce you to the practice. There is no charge, nor are donations requested.

    About us

    This center was created following the instruction of out teacher, Nyogen Roshi. We’ve been practicing for over 20 years under his instruction.
